Marguerite Swivel has been The Ergonomist's Ergonomics Correspondent for longer than the paper has existed. She files standing, on principle, and sitting, in practice. Her reporting on the 2019 monitor-arm shortage was described by one source as "a lot."

She does not take tips about posture. She has seen yours.
