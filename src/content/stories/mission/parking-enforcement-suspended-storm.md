The SFMTA isn't ticketing parking meters or time limits from Monday through Wednesday, and Thanksgiving is already a holiday with no enforcement, so the break runs through Thursday ([Mission Local](https://missionlocal.org/2026/11/san-francisco-ticketing-suspended-sfmta-storms-floods/)). Mayor Daniel Lurie asked anyone parked in a flood zone to move their car.

https://x.com/DanielLurie/status/2124342013667737850

You can still be ticketed for blocking a driveway or crosswalk, parking at a color curb, or street sweeping, unless Public Works calls off sweeping. This isn't Philadelphia.

## The tides

A coastal flood warning is in effect from 6 a.m. Monday through 4 p.m. Thursday ([CBS San Francisco](https://www.cbsnews.com/sanfrancisco/news/san-francisco-suspends-parking-enforcement-nov-23-25-flooding/)). The city's Department of Emergency Management says Wednesday's high tide could come close to the 1983 record at the San Francisco tide gauge, and the National Weather Service has forecast up to 2.7 feet of flooding above ground in low-lying areas ([Secret San Francisco](https://secretsanfrancisco.com/sf-king-tides-flooding-free-parking/)).
