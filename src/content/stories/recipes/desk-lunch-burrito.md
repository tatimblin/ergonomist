Every lunch is a desk lunch if you're brave enough, but this one was built for it. It's rolled tight enough to eat with one hand while the other stays on the mouse, and it's wrapped in foil, so the drip that every burrito eventually produces lands on aluminum instead of a keyboard. The Test Kitchen ate it through a 45-minute all-hands with its camera off, which is the only way the Test Kitchen attends all-hands. No one noticed. Rated four of five aldermen.

## Method

### For the beans

1. Heat the oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion and a pinch of salt and cook, stirring now and then, until soft and translucent, about 5 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and cumin and cook, stirring, until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
3. Add the beans and their liquid and bring to a simmer. Cook, mashing about half the beans with a fork or potato masher, until thick enough that a spoon dragged through leaves a trail, 8 to 10 minutes. Season with salt.

### To assemble

1. Warm a tortilla in a dry large skillet over medium-high heat until soft and pliable, about 20 seconds a side. Lay it on a sheet of foil.
2. Spread half the beans in a line across the lower third of the tortilla, leaving 2 inches clear at each side. Top with half the rice, cheese, salsa, avocado and sour cream.
3. Fold the sides in over the filling, then roll it up from the bottom, tucking the filling in with your fingers as you go and keeping it as tight as you can. Repeat with the second tortilla.
4. Put the burritos seam side down in the skillet over medium heat and toast until golden and the seam has sealed, 1 to 2 minutes. Turn and toast the other side.
5. Wrap each burrito tightly in foil. To eat, peel the foil back as you go.

## Notes

- Mashing half the beans is what holds the burrito together; whole beans roll out of the end.
- "Burrito-size" tortillas are 12 inches across. Anything smaller won't close around this much filling.

## Make Ahead and Storage

The beans keep, covered, for 4 days in the refrigerator. Rolled burritos, made without the avocado and sour cream, keep in their foil for 3 days refrigerated: reheat them, still wrapped, in a 400°F oven for 20 minutes, or unwrapped, under a damp paper towel, in the microwave for 2 minutes.

## Substitutions

Any canned bean works. Cheddar or pepper Jack can stand in for the Monterey Jack, and brown rice for white.
