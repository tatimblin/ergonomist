I used to live in Philadelphia, and I've had the best cheesesteaks there are. Dalessandro's and Abner's are the two best. This one, made from a single Trader Joe's run, is a top five.

## Method

### Bread

1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Split the demi-baguettes lengthwise without cutting all the way through.
2. Set them on a baking sheet and toast until warm and just crisp, 4 to 5 minutes. Turn off the oven and leave them in to stay warm.

### Vegetables

1. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the onion and a pinch of salt and cook, stirring now and then, until soft and starting to brown, about 6 minutes.
2. Add the mushrooms and cook, stirring, until they've given off their liquid and browned, about 5 minutes more. Move everything to one side of the pan.

### Steak

1. Add the beef to the empty side of the pan in a single layer. Season with salt and pepper and cook, breaking it up with two spatulas, until no longer pink, 2 to 3 minutes.
2. Chop the beef and vegetables together in the pan, then stir in the buffalo sauce.
3. Divide the mixture into two piles the length of the bread. Lay 3 slices of provolone over each and cover the pan until the cheese melts, about 1 minute.
4. Slide a spatula under each pile and turn it into a baguette. Eat right away.
