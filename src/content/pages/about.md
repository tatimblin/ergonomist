The Ergonomist is a satirical paper by [Tristan Timblin](/staff/tristan-timblin/).

Satire is labelled **Satire**, and reporting **Reported**. **Columns** are copied from [tristantimblin.dev](https://tristantimblin.dev). Real people and businesses named in satire did not do what the story says. See the [standards](/standards/).
