The Ergonomist was founded in 1487 by an ergonomic chair, which had opinions and nowhere to put them. For its first four centuries it was a single sheet tacked to the underside of a desk, read only by people who had dropped a pen. Circulation peaked in 1911 at nine, seven of them the same lumbar cushion. The paper has served the Mission since 2026, when someone finally told it where it was.

---

## What this actually is

None of the above is true. This part is.

The Ergonomist is a satirical paper written by [Tristan Timblin](/staff/tristan-timblin/). His own site is [tristantimblin.dev](https://tristantimblin.dev).

Stories are satire unless they're labelled **Reported**. Satire is made up: the events, the quotes, and everyone on the masthead but Tristan. A Reported story is real news, summarized in our own words, with a link to its source. **Columns** are Tristan's own posts, copied from tristantimblin.dev, where they first appear.

Any resemblance of a satirical story to real people or businesses is coincidental. A real business named in satire did not do what the story says it did.

What the paper won't write, how it corrects its mistakes, and how to have something taken down are in its [standards](/standards/).
