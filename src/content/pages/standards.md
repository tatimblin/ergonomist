## Satire

- Satire is labelled **Satire**.
- No invented crimes, health violations, closures, sexual conduct or drug use about a real named business or private person.

## Ethics

- Nothing about the editor's employer or its partners.
- AI-generated images are captioned as such.
- Reported stories are summarized in our own words and link their source.

## Corrections

Corrections are dated, at the foot of the story (the top if significant), and listed at [Corrections](/corrections/). Typos are fixed without a note.

## Takedowns

Named in a story and want it changed or removed? Get in touch; you'll hear back within 48 hours.
