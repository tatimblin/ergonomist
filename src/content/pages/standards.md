The Ergonomist is satire, but these are the rules it's written by, and they aren't a joke.

## Satire

Every story is satire unless it's labelled **Reported**. Columns are the editor's own posts, copied from [tristantimblin.dev](https://tristantimblin.dev).

- We never invent crimes, health violations, closures, sexual conduct or drug use about a real, named business or a private person.
- Satire about real places stays obviously absurd. No one should finish a story about a real street believing something happened there.
- Any resemblance of a satirical story to real people or businesses is coincidental. A real business named in satire did not do what the story says it did.

## Ethics

- We write nothing about the editor's employer or its partners.
- AI-generated images are captioned "Illustration (AI-generated)".
- A Reported story summarizes in our own words and links its source.
- Recipes are tested before they're published, and the method is our own.

## Corrections

When we get something wrong, we correct it. A correction goes at the foot of the story, dated; a significant one goes at the top. Typos, spelling and grammar are fixed without a note.

Every correction is also listed, newest first, at [Corrections](/corrections/), which has its own RSS feed. A satirical story can carry a satirical correction; it's part of the story, and as made up as the rest of it.

## Takedowns

If you run a business or you're a person named in The Ergonomist and you want it changed or taken down, write to us. You'll hear back within 48 hours. If the complaint is made in good faith, we'll unpublish the story or take your name out of it.
