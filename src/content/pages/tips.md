---
title: Send Us a Tip
description: How to send The Ergonomist a tip, a correction, or a complaint.
---

The Ergonomist relies on tips from readers, chairs, and the occasional desk. We read everything, and we protect our sources, mostly by forgetting who they were.

Other channels, none of which anyone checks:

- Leave a note under the third bench at Dolores Park. Not that one. The other third bench.
- Shout it into the fog at 4:15 p.m. The fog has no comment, but it is listening.
- Tell the standing desk on the fourth floor. It will rise to the occasion.

For anything real (a correction, a complaint, or something you'd like taken down), use the contact at the top of this page. How we handle each is in our [standards](/standards/).
